# Hylo Products & Pricing — Page Spec

**Version:** 1.0 — April 2026
**Companion to:** hylo-homepage-spec.md, hylo-stewardship-support-spec.md
**Purpose:** A single page that makes Hylo's product landscape legible: what's free (everything that matters), what's paid (and why), and how the paid products sustain the commons. The page needs to communicate a genuinely unusual structure — a platform where the core is free forever and the paid offerings exist to fund the infrastructure, not to gate the experience.

---

## The Structural Message

Most platforms use pricing pages to create anxiety: *you need the paid tier or you're missing out.* Feature grids are designed to make the free tier feel crippled. Hylo's situation is the opposite, and the page should reflect that.

The core message is: **Hylo is free. The full coordination platform — groups, conversations, events, requests and offers, projects, proposals, governance, the map, cross-group coordination — is free, and will stay free.** Paid products exist for groups with specific operational needs (more storage, video, API access, white-label deployment) and for organizations that want hands-on human support building their community. The money sustains an open-source commons.

This is a competitive advantage. Circle starts at $89/month. Mighty Networks starts at $49/month. Hylo's core is free. Say it plainly and let it land.

## Section 5 — Hylo Assistant

### Purpose

The Assistant is a different kind of product from Pro or Whitelabel. Pro gives groups more capacity. Whitelabel gives them independence. The Assistant gives them *intelligence* — a tool that understands the group's full context and helps stewards do work that would otherwise require hours of manual attention or simply not get done.

This is also the product most likely to raise eyebrows. AI in a commons-based, values-driven platform needs to be positioned carefully: it serves the group, not the platform. It doesn't surveil. It doesn't optimize for engagement. It helps stewards do the unglamorous work of keeping a community healthy — the work that determines whether a group thrives or goes silent.

### The Visual Weight Question

The Free section should feel *bigger* than any paid section. More features listed, more space, more visual weight. This is counterintuitive for a pricing page — usually the premium tier gets the spotlight. But Hylo's story is the opposite: the free tier IS the product, and the paid tiers are optional expansions. The visual hierarchy should reflect that.

Pro should feel like an exciting addition, not the thing you're missing. Assistant should feel like a genuinely helpful companion for stewards, not a surveillance tool or an engagement optimizer. Whitelabel should feel like a serious option for serious organizations, not an upsell from Pro.

### What This Page Does NOT Do

- **No feature gating on core coordination tools.** Groups, proposals, governance, map, cross-group coordination, requests and offers — all free. The comparison table should make this viscerally obvious.
- **No free trial language.** "Free forever" means free forever. No "free for 14 days" or "free up to X members."
- **No hidden pricing anywhere.** Every product has its price stated on the page. Free is free. Pro is $60/mo. Assistant is $20/mo. Whitelabel is $100/mo. No "contact sales" gatekeeping. The transparency is a values commitment — and a competitive advantage when competitors hide pricing behind demo requests.
- **No artificial progression.** The page shouldn't imply that groups "should" eventually upgrade. Some groups will use Hylo Free for years and that's a success, not a conversion failure.
- **No AI hype.** The Assistant section should not use words like "revolutionary," "game-changing," or "powered by AI" as a selling point in itself. AI is the mechanism, not the value proposition. The value is: your stewards can do better work with less overhead.

### Relationship Between This Page and Stewardship Support Page

This page includes a brief overview of stewardship support (Section 6) with a clear link to the dedicated page. The full pricing, deliverables, and philosophy live on the stewardship support page. This page is the map; that page is the territory.

## Open Questions

1. **Pro scope confirmation:** The spec assumes per-group pricing ($60/mo covers every member in the group). Confirm this is correct. Per-group is more commons-aligned and should be stated explicitly on the page.
2. **Pro storage amount:** "Expanded" storage is listed but no specific amount. What's the actual limit? 10 GB? 50 GB? Unlimited? Stating it matters — "expanded" is vague where the free tier is specific (1 GB).
3. **Whitelabel scope at $100/mo:** This is aggressively affordable for a whitelabel deployment. Does $100/mo include hosting, maintenance, and updates? Is there a setup fee? Are there limits on members or storage? The price will attract attention — make sure the scope is clearly defined so there's no mismatch between expectation and delivery.
4. **Whitelabel hosting model:** Hylo-hosted under a custom domain, or self-hosted by the client? Or both as options? This affects operational cost and the value proposition significantly.
5. **Assistant as add-on vs. standalone:** The spec positions Assistant ($20/mo) as something you add to any group — Free, Pro, or Whitelabel. Confirm this is correct. A group on the free tier paying $20/mo just for the Assistant should work without needing Pro.
6. **Assistant + Pro bundle:** If a group wants both Pro ($60) and Assistant ($20), is there a bundled price? $80/mo for both? Or does Pro include the Assistant? Keep it simple but think through the combinatorics.
7. **Free tier storage:** 1 GB per group — is that shared across all members, or per member? Per-group is simpler and more honest. State it clearly.
8. **Launch timing:** Pro and Whitelabel both say Summer 2026. Is that a firm commitment? If there's risk of slipping, "later this year" is safer than a specific season that becomes a broken promise.
9. **Assistant data boundaries:** The spec says "your group's data stays within your group." This needs to be technically precise. Does the RAG index include data from parent/child groups? From cross-posted content? From connected Google Drive folders that other groups also access?
10. **Assistant transparency:** "All AI interactions are transparent to stewards." What does this mean concretely? Can stewards see every question members ask the Assistant? Every answer it gives? Is there a log? The line between helpful transparency and surveillance of member queries is narrow.
11. **Moderation awareness configurability:** Can groups configure the sensitivity of moderation alerts? Can they turn moderation awareness off entirely? The values commitment section addresses the philosophy, but users will want mechanical specifics.
12. **Competitive pricing context:** Circle starts at $89/mo. Mighty Networks starts at $49/mo. Hylo Pro at $60/mo is in range but with a free tier that's more complete than either competitor's paid entry. Whitelabel at $100/mo is dramatically below market (custom white-label deployments typically run $500–$5,000+/mo). Make sure the team is comfortable sustaining these prices and that the economics work.
